How a mandate runs

Four stages, one accountable partner.

  1. Assess

    We review the business, the numbers and the options, and tell you plainly whether the transaction makes sense.

  2. Structure

    Valuation, instrument design, regulatory route with SECP and PSX, and the information memorandum.

  3. Market

    Investor and counterparty outreach, roadshows, book building and negotiation of terms.

  4. Close

    Documentation, approvals, settlement and execution through our brokerage where shares change hands.
