The firm

Boards and founders come to us when the decision matters most. We give independent, conflict-aware advice, a partner on every mandate, and a team that builds the model, drafts the documents and stays in the room until it closes.

Independent judgement

Our advice is not tied to a lending book. We say plainly when a transaction should not go ahead.

Partner-led, end to end

One accountable partner from assessment and structuring through marketing, approvals and close.

Advice that executes
